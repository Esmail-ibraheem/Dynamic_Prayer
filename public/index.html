Prayer blocks that follow the sun, not the clock.

Subscribe once in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. Every day’s blocks are worked out from your coordinates and your timezone, shown to colleagues as Busy, and kept inside your working hours. Your work account never gives this site any access.

Set up your feed

A recurring event is wrong almost every day

Prayer times move a few minutes daily with the sun and jump a full hour at each clock change. A fixed “daily at 13:00” event drifts away from Dhuhr, and an .ics file made with a fixed UTC offset is an hour off for half the year.

No permissions
It is a read-only webcal:// subscription (RFC 5545). Nothing connects to your Microsoft or Google account, so company OAuth policies don’t apply.
Right after every clock change
Each day is calculated on its own against the IANA timezone database, so CET and CEST, GMT and BST, EST and EDT are always applied to the correct dates.
Only during work
Blocks outside your working hours are left out. In winter Maghrib comes into the workday and is blocked; in summer it drops out again.
Same link when you travel
Change your city from a private link. The address in your work calendar never changes.
